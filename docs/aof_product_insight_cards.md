# AOF Product Insight Cards

## Purpose

AOF leak detection must be readable as a product surface, not only as operator debug output.

The backend may detect many short-stack decision candidates, but Hero needs the surface to answer:

- What is the first AOF pattern I should review?
- Why does it matter strategically?
- What should I change next session?
- What should I avoid over-correcting?

## Surface Rule

`Leak candidates` is a count, not an insight.

The operator surface should lead with:

1. Lead Read
2. Next Adjustment
3. Cooler Guard
4. Priority Leak Cards
5. Raw operator evidence behind details

Raw JSON remains available because deep operator mode is still active, but it should not be the first thing Hero has to parse.

## Priority Card Meaning

Each priority card is grouped by:

- mistake family,
- hand class,
- repeat count,
- situation mix,
- format mix,
- stack-band mix,
- actual result mix,
- and concrete example hands.

The card then adds product language:

- severity,
- why it matters,
- what to change,
- what not to overreact to,
- evidence line,
- and operator review status.

## Guardrail

AOF insight cards are not solver-grade EV truth.

They are deterministic post-hoc interpretation cards that help Hero find repeated personal short-stack implementation patterns. Premium lost all-ins and standard calloffs remain protected from fake correction by the cooler guardrail.
